# Equity reference-series qualification

Research checked: **2026-10-18**. Scope: the selected MSCI World, MSCI Emerging Markets, MSCI ACWI Small Cap Value, MSCI Europe Small Cap, MSCI USA Small Cap references, and the approved secondary MSCI Europe Small Cap Value definition. This records evidence and qualification status; it does not authorize an alternate index, ETF proxy, or class-history extension.

## Outcome

**None of the six definitions is qualified as a usable production series.** Primary documents establish the intended index identities and several return conventions, but this investigation did not establish an exact provider series identifier for the required EUR-net levels, licensed/permitted application access, an accessible daily payload, or usable end-to-end coverage. Therefore no mapping is enabled and no observations should be represented as verified. The existing target-only definitions remain the honest runtime state.

The inability to prove a complete input is not a claim that MSCI does not offer the data. It is a qualification blocker: obtain the exact series identifiers and dated entitlement/access terms from MSCI or an authorized distributor, then validate the returned series and boundaries before enabling it.

## Passive-class convention check

The exact iShares documents resolve the selected **iShares** passive classes' comparison conventions: IE000ZYRH0Q7 tracks **MSCI World Index Net (EUR)** and IE000QAZP7L2 tracks **MSCI Emerging Markets Net Returns (EUR)**. Both are accumulating EUR classes, USD-base funds, and are not the separately identified hedged classes; a EUR class denomination does not hedge underlying currency exposure. Their exact class start date is **2025-08-21**. This does not verify Vanguard classes IE00B03HD191 or IE0031786696: their exact return variants and hedging conventions remain unresolved for this qualification. These exact-class facts do not establish a series vendor key, license, daily index payload, or permission to extend the iShares classes' history to their older fund inception dates. [W1–W3; E1–E3; B1]

## Source and access findings

1. MSCI's official index-data search describes selections for market, currency, size, index level (price/total/net), style, and date, and directs users to contact MSCI Client Service for additional historical data. The page is a discovery interface, not proof that this project's account has access to a particular downloadable history. [D1]
2. MSCI's current Notice & Disclaimer reserves proprietary rights and says prior written permission is required for several uses including commercial use, third-party services, redistribution/resale, benchmark use in financial products, database population, and unauthorized automated extraction. This is a documented restriction, not a legal opinion on this application's status; no applicable license, contract, or written permission was produced for this work. [L1]
3. Public factsheets/pages retrieved in earlier research provide dated index definitions and summarized history, not a complete daily source response. No credentials were used and no private or account-restricted endpoint was accessed. No public network is used by tests.
4. Exact index-code identifiers are available for Europe Small Cap (106233) and USA Small Cap (106229). These are official identity codes only; the exact code/key for each selected EUR-net (or USD-net plus FX) return stream remains unverified. For World, Emerging Markets, ACWI Small Cap Value, and Europe Small Cap Value, this investigation did not establish a provider series key suitable for the application. Do not infer one from an ETF ticker, display name, or generic data-search selector.

## Required evidence to clear each blocker

For each mapping independently: (1) provider/distributor-issued exact series identifier and variant (net, currency, hedging); (2) dated evidence of access rights permitting the intended retrieval, caching, comparison and display; (3) a non-secret sample/payload or documented feed contract that identifies observation dates, values, cadence and correction/revision policy; (4) start/end coverage with launch versus back-test boundary stated; and (5) reconciliation proving return basis, currency conversion, no double adjustment, and immutable completed-date handling. For passive funds, separately preserve the verified exact-class inception boundary; extending history needs verified same-class continuity or explicit approval for another substitution, neither of which is available here.
