# Twelve / Swiss Re cat-bond reference qualification

**Evidence checked:** 18 October 2026. **Status: not qualified for usable comparison.**
The approved target definition remains Swiss Re Global Cat Bond Total Return Index
(SRGLTRR); this note does not approve a substitute or claim that a usable series is
available. No source Adapter or production behavior is enabled.

## Findings

| Gate | Evidence and conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| Exact Twelve class and currency hedge | **Unresolved.** No current primary legal document establishing the hedge policy of Twelve Cat Bond P EUR Acc, ISIN IE00BD2B9710, was available in the inspected official materials. The current Twelve Securis products page is a strategy overview, not an exact-class prospectus/KID. The prior exact-class check also found the former Twelve products URL redirected to the current site without exposing class documents. EUR class denomination does not establish a EUR hedge. |
| Selected series identity and return treatment | **Partially confirmed.** Swiss Re's methodology landing page, published 1 August 2014, says its Cat Bond Performance Indices are designed to reflect catastrophe-bond market returns and calls them the first sector total-return indices, launched in 2007. Swiss Re's *Insurance-Linked Securities Market Insights*, February 2025, names the Swiss Re Global Cat Bond Total Return Index and SRGLTRR, describes returns incorporating coupons and marks, and characterizes Swiss Re cat-bond indices as based on indicative pricing. This identifies a plausible selected series and broad total-return treatment, but does not establish its currency variant, exact mechanics, current licensed payload, or compatibility with this class. |
| Observation cadence and usable history | **Unresolved; daily is not established.** Twelve's 2023 *Multi Asset Approach* paper describes weekly calculation for the cat-bond index. This is historical evidence, not proof of current publication cadence. The 2014 methodology page exposes a publication request form rather than the methodology itself. The 2025 market-insights publication supplies no documented current observation schedule or complete usable history. Current Twelve Securis web material includes dated cat-bond market updates, but those are not SRGLTRR index observations and cannot establish daily series access. |
| Permitted access | **Unresolved.** Public Swiss Re materials inspected identify/describe the index but do not grant access or a right to retrieve and use the full index history. The full methodology is behind a publication-request flow; no request or contact was made. Public mentions and ticker identity do not prove data entitlement. |

## Admission decision and next evidence needed

Do not enable SRGLTRR as a usable component for IE00BD2B9710. The target remains
selected but unqualified because exact-class hedging, compatible series currency,
current cadence, accessible historical observations and permitted use have not been
verified. The existing comparison gate must therefore leave fund-relative results
unavailable with a precise limitation; it must not turn weekly marks into daily
independent risk observations, fill internal gaps for risk calculations, shorten the
comparison window, or substitute HY credit, cash, another class, or another index.

To reconsider admission, obtain (a) a dated current prospectus/KID or other manager
primary disclosure explicitly resolving IE00BD2B9710 hedge policy and return history,
and (b) Swiss Re's current full methodology plus a documented access/entitlement
route and sample or complete SRGLTRR observations showing currency, return convention,
dates, coverage and actual cadence. If observations are weekly, a human must decide
whether a separately specified weekly comparison is acceptable; the existing daily
planning assumption is not authorization to change that cadence.
